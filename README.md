# Maltra Food Grocer — Finance & Accounting Portfolio

## Project overview

This project presents a fictional Australian food and consumer-goods business case designed to demonstrate practical accounting, financial reporting and process improvement capabilities relevant to an Accountant role.

The case study simulates a month-end close and explores how an accounting team can improve reporting accuracy, balance sheet controls, inventory reconciliation and capital expenditure oversight.

## Skills demonstrated

* Month-end close and financial reporting
* General ledger and balance sheet reconciliations
* Actual-versus-budget variance analysis
* Inventory and cost of goods sold (COGS)
* Fixed asset register and depreciation
* Capital expenditure (CAPEX) tracking
* Multi-entity and intercompany reconciliation
* Audit support and reporting governance
* Excel-based financial analysis
* Power BI-ready financial data
* Finance process improvement and responsible AI use

## Project deliverables

| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Business scenario

Maltra Food Grocer is a fictional multi-entity food and consumer-goods group. The case study examines a September 2026 reporting period and investigates financial variances, reconciliation exceptions, inventory controls and finance process improvement opportunities.

## Tools and techniques

Microsoft Excel, Power BI-ready datasets, financial modelling, reconciliation controls and structured month-end reporting.
